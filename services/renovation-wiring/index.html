Renovation wiring in Orillia.

A bathroom renovation in an Orillia home is a chance to match the wiring to how the room is now used, including the household's morning and evening rush. Settle the electrical scope before tiling starts.

Electrical decisions belong early in the renovation

A renovation changes how the space will be used and what equipment it must serve. Provide the drawings and appliance specifications before the electrical scope is finalized. A Licensed Electrical Contractor can assess retained wiring, new circuits and the proposed loads. Municipal building permissions and ESA electrical notification are separate matters, and the project may require both.

Prepare the scope for your Orillia property

List every item the new bathroom will use, such as lights, a fan, a heated floor or a mirror with a built-in light, and gather their product sheets. Note which family members use the room at the same time.

Ask which parts of the existing wiring the contractor expects to keep, and why. Ask how the electrical work will be scheduled around the plumber and tiler, and who handles the ESA notification before walls are closed.

What should the assessment establish?

Every item the renovated Orillia bathroom will hold is named in the scope, the lighting, the fan and any heated items included, with a note of those that need a circuit or protection of their own. A shorter list separates the wiring that stays from the wiring that is replaced. A section from the contractor explains where the electrical work has to be seen and completed before surfaces are finished. How the ESA notification and any municipal permissions are handled, as two separate matters, is the subject of the remaining section.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

When should the electrical work happen in a bathroom renovation?

Usually before surfaces are finished, because wiring is harder to change once walls and floors are closed. Ask the contractor to place the electrical stages in the overall schedule with the other trades.

Can a heated floor or fan share a circuit with the bathroom lights?

The equipment and the existing wiring settle it, and the decision is the contractor's after assessment. Share the product sheets so the proposal states which items share a circuit and which are separate.

Useful project details

  • Construction schedule and other trades

Only photograph labels and locations that you can see safely. Do not remove electrical covers or approach damaged equipment to gather information.
